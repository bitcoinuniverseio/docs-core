This feature is in development. Availability depends on the running Core build
and the selected agent's permissions.

Open **Assist** from Portfolio, Explorer, Market or pending activity. Review the
address and network, describe a goal, then choose **Prepare**. The review shows what
the app can actually do before you start it.

Supported reads include Bitcoin balances, market listings, pending operations
and transaction progress. A balance result covers Bitcoin only; it does not
establish token or inscription holdings. Missing sources remain unavailable.

Tasks retain progress on the server. You can pause, resume or cancel future
work. Watching a transaction stops after confirmation or a limited monitoring
period. Confirmation is distinct from settlement and indexing.

If inference is unavailable, supported deterministic reads remain available.
Other goals direct you to the relevant form. Listing, purchase and inscription
forms still require their existing review and wallet authorization. A plan does
not sign or broadcast a transaction.

Files stay in the local capture flow unless a supported form explicitly accepts
them. Opening Inscribe is a handoff, not a completed inscription. Keep the
receipt from the application that submits the operation.
